Accra, Ghana

The Ghana flag features three equal horizontal bands of red (top), gold (middle), and green (bottom), with a central black five-pointed star in the gold stripe. The red symbolizes the blood of martyrs for independence, the gold represents mineral wealth, the green signifies the lush forests, and the black star symbolizes African emancipation and unity. The flag was designed by Theodosia Okoh and adopted at Ghana's independence in 1957.
